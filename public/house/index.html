How I read the plan

  • The scan is upside down. Turned the right way, east is at the top and roads run along the east and north sides. The main door faces east, into the north-east corner.
  • The footprint comes from the room sizes: 11′3″ + 12′0″ across, 9′0″ + 11′0″ + 12′0″ deep. Setbacks are 6′ north, 2′6″ south and 3′ west as written. The east front yard has no figure, so I scaled it from the drawing at about 8′.
  • The dotted lines between the front room, hall and dining are open spans with a beam overhead, so those three spaces flow into one.
  • Page 2 lists 6 doors, 8 windows, 2 ventilators and 6 cupboards. The model has the same counts; the two bedroom cupboards are the lofts drawn as dashed lines on the west wall.
  • Heights are not on the plan. I used a 2′ plinth, 10′ ceilings, 7′ door and window heads, 9″ outer walls and a 2′9″ terrace parapet.
  • The circle on the north side is the septic tank, next to the outside toilet. It is modelled as precast concrete rings with a slab cover and a vent pipe, like the old tank in your first photo. The ⊕ marked బోరింగ్ is the borewell hand pump.

  • An arugu, the raised sitting platform, outside the north gate with two chairs, on the open strip beside the road.
  • Open land of about 10′ between the north wall and the road, the south neighbour well away, and nothing behind the house. These gaps are estimates; tell me the real figures and I will match them.

Worth checking with your mason

  • The staircase covers the kitchen window. Both flights run along the east wall, so the kitchen window looks into the underside of the stair. Ask about open-riser or cantilevered steps, or add a window on the kitchen’s south wall with an exhaust fan.
  • East setback depth. The stair is about 5′ wide. Confirm the front yard is at least 8′ so a walkway stays clear beside it.
  • Toilet access. The only indoor toilet opens from the master bedroom. Everyone else uses the outside toilet on the north side.
  • Puja room width. At 4′ wide with a platform on the east wall, the clear floor inside is about 3′6″ × 7′.
  • Septic tank and borewell distance. They are about 28′ (8.5 m) apart on this plan. Common guidance is to keep a septic tank or soak pit around 15 m (50′) from a drinking-water borewell, which this plot cannot fit. Ask about a fully sealed tank and where its outflow will soak away, and keep that away from the borewell.
  • The coconut palm in your photos. I could not tell where it stands on the plot, so the palms here are placed at the corners. If it falls inside the footprint it will have to come down.
